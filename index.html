The Last Spectacle

Debord, the Internet, and the Age of Generative Reality

The spectacle did not disappear. It learned to generate.

Read / Download the PDF See what is inside

A modern rereading of Debord for the age of generative reality.

This book follows the spectacle from representation to broadcast media, from the internet to platforms, from algorithmic reality to generative AI. It is not an AI trend report. It is a systems diagnosis of how images, interfaces, and generated realities now shape what people see, trust, remember, and act on.
